Printer's Gazette & Inquiry

Where are the files deposited?

Inside standard filesystem directories on your personal drives as human-readable CommonMark files.

Can existing folders be ingested?

Yes. Drag your current library directory directly onto Margin to begin immediate marginal reading.

Is an internet telegraph line required?

No. Margin is built for completely severed, air-gapped workstations without connectivity.

How do notes survive manuscript revisions?

Cryptographic block digests anchor marginal notes to sentence phrases rather than brittle line counts.
