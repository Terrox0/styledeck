Marginalia & Reflection

Where thoughtful reading meets permanent marginal commentary.

Modern note applications treat your thoughts like detached warehouse inventory. Margin restores the centuries-old tradition of the scholia: placing your private reflections, critiques, and citations directly in the outer perimeter of your source documents.

Content-Addressed Blocks

Marginal annotations anchor to document paragraphs via cryptographic block digests, surviving text edits without detachment.

Inquiries & Clarifications

Section III · Colloquy

Where are my files persisted?

Directly inside your designated filesystem directories as plain CommonMark text files. You retain permanent ownership of all notes.

Can I import existing markdown libraries?

Yes. Margin ingests existing directories instantly without mutating your formatting or nesting schemes.

Does the application require network access?

Never. The core software is fully functional air-gapped without an active internet connection.

How do annotations survive text revisions?

Content-addressed block hashing anchors marginal notes to linguistic sentences rather than fragile line numbers.
