Dialogue & Clarity

III

Where is scholarship preserved?

Exclusively on your local machine in designated POSIX directories as clean, standard markdown files.

Can existing archives be imported?

Margin ingests your established markdown libraries instantly without modifying formatting or folders.

Does Margin require telecommunication?

Never. Margin is designed for private air-gapped workstations operating without network access.

How do margin anchors endure revisions?

Content-addressed block hashes anchor notes to underlying sentence structures rather than fragile line numbers.
