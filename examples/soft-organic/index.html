Gentle Answers

Frequently pondered queries

Where do my notes rest?

On your local computer in folders you designate, saved as standard human-readable text.

Can I import my existing library?

Yes. Drag your markdown folder into Margin and begin reading without conversion rituals.

Is continuous connection required?

Never. Margin is completely self-contained and thrives in peaceful, offline isolation.

How do margin anchors stay intact?

Linguistic block hashes attach your marginal comments directly to sentences, surviving paragraph edits.
